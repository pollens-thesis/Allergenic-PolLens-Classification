## Notes on paper-only scope

- The paper's UI description (Section 5.1.3, Figs. 10–18) describes a **high-contrast light theme** with white backgrounds and green accents for the Figma prototype, but Section 5.1.1.3 (Non-Functional Requirements) and Section 5.1.1.2 (Geospatial Mapping) separately specify a **dark theme** / "dark-mode interactive map" and dark-mode bounding-box visibility. This is an internal inconsistency within the paper itself (not a paper-vs-frontend conflict) and should be flagged during reconciliation.
- Software/Hardware tool versions (Table I, Table II) and the CS Thesis Workplan (Table XI, spanning March–October 2026) are operational/planning details, not user-facing features, and are omitted from the feature table above but noted here for completeness.
- Comparison-of-algorithms metrics (mAP@50/75 for YOLOv11; F1/Precision/Recall for ResNet34) are prototype-stage benchmark results reported in the paper, not shipped product features.
